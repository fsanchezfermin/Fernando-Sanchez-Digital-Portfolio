---
title: Room modeler and its use in understanding acoustics
date: 2026-10-07
---
This is my first project documented on this site. I will update this document as I do more progress on my software. 
The idea is to develop software that can allow the user to design rooms of different dimensions and materials digitally and listen to how sound spreads in such a room. The reverberation in the specific room will be computed through a delay line, with delay times and amplifications set by raytracing from a designated (and user-modifiable) pair of "source" and "sinks" in the digital room, where the sound will be originated and listened to respectively.
The main goal with this software is to give students an intuition for how sounds spread in a room depending on the room dimensions and materials, as well as how rooms can be made to be more or less reverberating using the physics of sound and some geometry. Additionally, the development of the software will be available for anyone curious of how the physics and math of sound factor in to making such a model.
